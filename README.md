# Nothing Inc. — The Daily Nothing

Open index.html in your browser to play. The entire site, artwork and BMX game are contained in that one file; no installation or external assets are required.

Deliver newspapers to the yellow letterboxes on the left during a 30-second paper round. Arrow keys steer; Space throws left; P pauses. On phones, hold the steering buttons and tap Throw Paper. Tap the game area to resume after a pause.

Deliveries earn 10 points, broken windows lose 2, and crashes lose 5. Your best score is saved in your browser when local storage is available. Switching away pauses the game.

To host, upload index.html to your static web host or GitHub Pages repository. Keep your existing email DNS records when connecting your domain.
